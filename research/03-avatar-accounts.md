# Avatar and synthetic presenters

Avatar slice. Retrieved 31 Oct 2026.

Status: ISSUES. Named synthetic presenters have sourced reach or sales, but the verified money is in a Chinese digital-twin livestream, an Instagram photo persona, a Twitch AI VTuber, and a motion-capture K-pop group, not in an original photoreal host on TikTok, Reels, or YouTube Shorts.

## Findings

### Luo Yonghao and Xiao Mu (Zhu Xiaomu), digital twins — Baidu Youxuan

Operator: Luo Yonghao and co-host Xiao Mu, through Be Friends (交个朋友). On-screen people were interactive digital copies of those two real hosts, not an original fictional person.

- Platform: Baidu e-commerce livestream “Youxuan,” Sunday, June 15, 2025. Not Douyin. CNBC reported the human “Be Friends” Douyin account at nearly 24.7 million followers, and Luo’s comment on the session to 1.7 million Weibo followers.
- Niche: livestream shopping (electronics, food, and other goods).
- Tool: Baidu’s generative model, trained on five years of their videos to copy jokes and style (Wu Jialu, head of research at Be Friends Holding, to CNBC, June 2025). Chinese coverage names Baidu’s Huibo Star (慧播星) product.
- Cadence: one documented session, well over six hours. A company press release describes 24-hour capability; that is a product claim, not a verified schedule.
- Structure: two avatars co-hosting, explaining products and answering in real time, with Luo’s mannerisms. The same press release says the system called a knowledge base 13,000 times and generated 97,000 characters of product talk. That is a company self-report.
- Disclosure: presented as a digital-human debut. Luo told Weibo it was his first time selling with a virtual human and that the effect had startled him (CNBC translation).
- Monetization: gross merchandise value, not a creator ad rate. Commission is not in these sources.
- Metrics: Baidu told CNBC the session did 55 million yuan ($7.65 million) and that Luo’s own prior Youxuan livestream in May 2025, just over four hours, had fewer orders in electronics, food, and other key categories. China Daily (July 28, 2025) gives the same session as more than 13 million views and over 55 million yuan. Be Friends’ June 22, 2025 press release says more than 13 million views, over 55 million yuan, and 6.5 hours, and claims GMV in the first 26 minutes beat one hour of Luo’s human livestream. That comparison is a company self-report. CNBC’s headline says he earned more than in the human stream; the article body does not publish the human stream’s GMV.

Douyin rule, same interview: Wu said Douyin had restricted virtual hosts, especially when they do not interact with viewers. No date for that rule, and this session was not on Douyin. No source here shows this account banned.

### Liu Qiangdong digital human — JD livestream

Single source: a China Daily column, July 28, 2025. It says a digital human of JD founder Liu Qiangdong appeared in JD livestream rooms “last April,” so April 2025, selling groceries and appliances, with over 20 million views in the first hour and 50 million yuan in sales for the session. Tool, handle, disclosure label, and cadence are not in that column. Treat as one newspaper column, not a company filing.

### Aitana López — Instagram @fit_aitana

Operator: The Clueless, Barcelona (Rubén Cruz; Diana Núñez). Synthetic fitness and lifestyle persona. Sources describe photos (gym, fashion, cosplay, brand posts, paid lingerie photos), not a spoken short-form script. Adjacent to a talking presenter, not a confirmed one.

- Disclosure: Infobae (Oct. 9, 2026) says the Instagram profile presents as “perfil generado con IA” and as virtual fitness and lifestyle tied to The Clueless.
- Monetization and metrics, all operator-side or secondary:
  - Self-report, November 2023: Cruz told Euronews, as reported by Fortune (Nov. 23, 2023), about €3,000 a month on average and €10,000 in one month, mostly social ads, plus a sports-supplement ambassador deal. Fortune also reported just over €1,000 per advert in that Euronews account, and 122,000 Instagram followers at the time.
  - Infobae (Oct. 9, 2026): more than €4,000 a month from lingerie photos on Fanvue; subscription €15 a month; more than 4,000 Fanvue followers; 404,000 Instagram followers and 376 Instagram posts. Fanvue posts more often than Instagram. Brand deals are mentioned and not priced.
  - Self-report via UNILAD (May 21, 2026): a spokesperson said brand deals, Fanvue, OpenArt, and a skincare brand, Vellum, add up to about $50,000–$80,000 a month. Tabloid. See Conflicts.
- A follower-count site’s “sponsorship value” estimate is not earnings. Not used.

### Neuro-sama — Twitch vedal987

Operator: Vedal (Vedal987). Anime-style AI VTuber: a large language model speaks and plays games. Not photoreal, and not a TikTok, Reels, or Shorts account. KAI-YOU (Dec. 24, 2025) describes a local LLM that answers chat, plays games, and sings.

- Debut on Twitch: Dec. 19, 2022 (GameSpot). Birthday subathons around that date.
- Disclosure: marketed as an AI VTuber. No source here of a platform synthetic-media strike.
- Reach: Tubefilter (Jan. 2, 2025) said the channel was near 700,000 subscribers and live with about 21,000 concurrent viewers.
- Metrics are viewer spend, then journalist arithmetic, not take-home pay:
  - Tubefilter, on the Jan. 1, 2025 Hype Train: level 111, 84,904 subs bought, 1,201,225 Bits. Its estimate of nearly $212,000 in subs plus $12,000 in Bits assumes a 50/50 Twitch split and all Tier-1 subs.
  - GameSpot, on the December 2025 third-birthday subathon: level 120, 118,989 subscriptions, 1,000,073 Bits.
  - KAI-YOU (Dec. 24, 2025): more than 110,000 subs and more than 1 million Bits, converted by the outlet to about 90 million yen, and it warns that figure is not creator revenue. It also says level 123. See Conflicts.

### PLAVE — VLAST (motion capture, not a generated person)

Five virtual members (Yejun, Noah, Bambi, Eunho, Hamin), debut March 2023. News Epoch reports that real members sing, dance, and talk; motion capture and Unreal Engine drive the characters. They are synthetic on screen and human off screen. Weak match for a generated presenter. Included because the on-screen bodies are virtual and the sales are documented.

- 2025 album sales: 2,353,570 copies (Hanteo News). Caligo Pt.1, released Feb. 3, 2025: first-week sales 1,038,308 (Korea Times, Feb. 10, 2025, Vlast citing Hanteo) or 1,039,308 (Korea Herald, citing Hanteo). “Dash” debuted at No. 195 on the Billboard Global 200 (Korea Herald, citing Vlast). Korea Times: over 11 million Melon streams in the first 24 hours.
- Concerts, 2025: 12 shows in six Asian cities, 126,018 people; November Gocheok Sky Dome, about 33,000 over two days, described as the first virtual-artist solo concert there (Hanteo News).
- Company results: News Epoch says VLAST’s 2025 revenue was 85.51 billion won, up 88.4% from 45.39 billion in 2024; operating profit 20.2 billion won; goods 56.79 billion won (66.4%); services 28.72 billion won. It attributes this to the audit report. This file was not opened here. Social handle was not in the sources used.

### Platform rules (not accounts)

YouTube disclosure, official blog, May 27, 2026. Realistic or meaningfully altered AI must still be disclosed by the creator, a requirement the post dates to 2024. The photoreal label moves under the player on long videos and onto the video on Shorts. From May 2026, YouTube may auto-label significant photoreal AI if the creator does not set a disclosure. Some labels cannot be removed (YouTube’s own tools such as Veo or Dream Screen, and fully generative C2PA metadata). The post says a disclosure label alone does not change recommendations or whether a video can earn money.

YouTube monetization, Help Center note dated July 15, 2025, plus a YouTube Community reply that month. “Repetitious content” was renamed “inauthentic content.” Mass-produced, generic, repetitive, or templated content stays ineligible for the YouTube Partner Program. YouTube said this is not a new ban and not an AI ban: channels using AI can still monetize, and realistic altered or synthetic content must be disclosed. Examples of mass-produced content in the Community reply: narrated stories with only superficial differences, and slideshows that share the same narration. Reused-content rules for commentary, clips, compilations, and reactions were unchanged.

Instagram, official creator blog, Aug. 31, 2026. The “AI creator” test label was renamed “AI-generated profile.” It is for a profile whose person is AI-generated, not for a human who uses AI tools. Unlabeled profiles that Instagram detects as an AI person can be removed from recommendations until they add the label or win an appeal. Profiles that add the label are told their reach will not change because of the label.

TikTok, official safety page (no date on the excerpt) and a TikTok Shop seller page (no date on the excerpt). Creators must label realistic AI scenes or people. TikTok auto-labels its own AI effects and some C2PA uploads. The seller page says failing to disclose significant AI can lead to removal or account restrictions, and that turning on the AI toggle is not itself a demotion if other rules are met. No named account was shown being punished.

## Patterns

Company-reported, interactive digital twins of famous hosts are the only cases here with eight-figure yuan sales tied to a synthetic person speaking a sales script. Luo’s June 15, 2025 session (Baidu, via CNBC) and Liu’s April 2025 JD session (China Daily only) are the pair. Both are likenesses of real celebrities, not unknown fictional faces.

Where a fictional or original synthetic person shows up, the sourced money is not short-form ads. Aitana’s public figures are Instagram and Fanvue photos. Neuro-sama’s public figures are Twitch subscriptions and Bits. PLAVE’s public figures are albums, goods, and concerts by human performers in virtual bodies.

2026 disclosure rules split by platform. YouTube (May 27, 2026) said the label itself does not change recommendations or monetization. Instagram (Aug. 31, 2026) said an unlabeled AI-person profile can lose recommendations. Both still want the synthetic person identified.

## Conflicts

Aitana’s income has three incompatible public versions. Fortune/Euronews, November 2023: about €3,000 in a typical month and €10,000 once, mostly ads (agency self-report). Infobae, Oct. 9, 2026: more than €4,000 a month on Fanvue, and more than 4,000 Fanvue followers at €15 a month. UNILAD, May 21, 2026: a spokesperson self-report of about $50,000–$80,000 a month across brands, Fanvue, OpenArt, and Vellum. Arithmetic tension inside Infobae: 4,000 paying subscribers at €15 would be €60,000 gross before fees, well above “more than €4,000.” The articles do not say how many of those followers pay.

CNBC’s June 19, 2025 headline and key points say Luo’s avatar earned more than his previous human livestream. The body only says Baidu reported fewer orders in key categories on that human stream, and it does not give the human GMV. The “we beat the human hour” claim is in Be Friends’ press release.

Neuro-sama’s December 2025 Hype Train is level 120 with 118,989 subs and 1,000,073 Bits in GameSpot, and about level 123 with “more than 110,000 subs” in KAI-YOU. Tubefilter’s ~$224,000 and KAI-YOU’s ~90 million yen are outlet calculations. KAI-YOU says its yen figure is not what Vedal kept.

PLAVE’s Caligo Pt.1 first week is 1,038,308 copies (Korea Times, citing Vlast and Hanteo) and 1,039,308 (Korea Herald, citing Hanteo). Statista’s 2025 album chart is paywalled and uses Circle Chart, so it was not compared.

YouTube’s July 15, 2025 monetization note and its May 27, 2026 label post do not contradict each other. The label is not the penalty. Mass-produced or templated channels are ineligible for monetization whether or not they use AI. Instagram’s reach limit is for missing the profile label, which YouTube’s label post does not mirror.

## Inferences

Inference: a lab that needs a photoreal person to deliver a script on TikTok, Reels, or Shorts does not have a public 2024–2026 case in this search with an independent revenue number. The closest spoken-script money is Luo’s Baidu livestream, and that used a famous man’s face, voice, and archive on a platform that allowed interactive digital hosts.

Inference: Douyin, as Wu described it to CNBC, is a poor default for a non-interactive avatar even if the same avatar can sell on Baidu Youxuan. No effective date was given.

Inference: on Instagram after Aug. 31, 2026, an unlabeled photoreal AI profile is the distribution risk. On YouTube, the May 27, 2026 post says the label is not that risk; the July 15, 2025 inauthentic-content rule is the monetization risk if videos are interchangeable templates.

Inference: Aitana’s follower growth (about 122,000 in November 2023 to about 404,000 by Oct. 9, 2026) does not show that spoken Reels made the money. The articles that price her describe ads and photos.

Inference: Neuro-sama’s subathon totals do not transfer to a scripted short-form ad channel. Viewers were paying a live AI personality they already followed.

Inference: PLAVE’s 85.51 billion won is evidence for virtual characters performed by people, not for a generated face reading a script.
