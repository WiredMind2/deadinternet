# What already works

You want a lab that tries many short videos and keeps the ones that make money. As of 31 Oct 2026, the public record points at faceless YouTube Shorts. The operators who named a dollar figure were paid a few cents to about 30 Canadian cents per thousand views, and the months they called profitable sat on tens or hundreds of millions of views. Avatar tools can make the file. This search did not find an original photoreal host with verified revenue on TikTok, Reels, or Shorts.

Every dollar figure below is an operator or company claim unless the sentence says an outside counter measured it. Slice notes live next to this file. This page is the judgment.

## Slice results

- Public projects. ISSUES. Official post and view docs are real. No dated operator report of a generate, approve, then post workflow. InVideo was not opened on an official page.
- Faceless accounts. ISSUES. Named channels and view counts are sourced. The dollar figures are operator claims, and a course page contradicts an interview.
- Avatar accounts. ISSUES. Sourced money sits in a celebrity livestream, a photo persona, a Twitch AI VTuber, and a motion-capture group.
- Craft. ISSUES. Ranking pages name an early hook and continued watching. TikTok's live pages timed out. Length studies disagree with the three-minute product caps. A caption study's own tests were mostly not significant.
- Money and tests. ISSUES. Program rules are on official pages. TikTok's own pages disagree on the cash-out floor. No method-backed short-video affiliate conversion rate turned up.

## Tools stop at a file

ShortGPT, the current MoneyPrinter README, SaarD00's generator, and Vsub can take a topic or a script and write a vertical file with voice and captions. HeyGen's API returns an avatar MP4. Synthesia's video API returns a scripted avatar file on Pro or above. None of those pages pull revenue, and none describe a human approval step. A caller can sit between the file and a later publish call. That is how you compose the lab's gate. It is not a feature those products ship. [ShortGPT](https://github.com/RayVentura/ShortGPT) [HeyGen](https://developers.heygen.com/docs/quick-start) [Synthesia](https://developers.synthesia.io/docs/video-api-introduction) [Vsub](https://vsub.io/docs/api/ai-videos)

The official post APIs are separate, and they are tight.

TikTok Direct Post is `POST /v2/post/publish/video/init/` at 6 requests per minute per user token. Unaudited clients may post only as private, and only for up to 5 users in 24 hours. TikTok says the shared creator cap is typically around 15 posts a day and may vary. `is_aigc: true` adds the platform AI tag. The query API can return view and engagement counts for up to 20 video IDs. It does not return revenue. [TikTok Direct Post](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-direct-post) [TikTok sharing guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines) [TikTok video query](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/tiktok-api-v2-video-query)

Instagram publishing is two calls for a professional account. Create a Reel container, then publish it. Meta's page, updated 20 Oct 2026, states both a 100-post cap and a 50-post cap in 24 hours. Containers expire in 24 hours. The page does not document views or revenue. [Instagram content publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)

YouTube `videos.insert` defaults to 100 uploads a day. The Analytics API documents `views` and `estimatedRevenue`. Estimated revenue is net, estimated, and adjusted at month end. It is the only per-video revenue field these pages document. [YouTube quota](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/determine_quota_cost) [YouTube Analytics metrics](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/metrics)

Current MoneyPrinter tells the operator to paste a TikTok `sessionid` cookie. That is not the Content Posting API. MoneyPrinterTurbo's README claims a direct upload to TikTok, Instagram, and Shorts. A later release note mentions Upload-Post handles. The publisher source was not read, so the upload remains a maintainer claim. [MoneyPrinter README](https://github.com/FujiwaraChoki/MoneyPrinter) [MoneyPrinterTurbo](https://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo)

HeyGen's own pages disagree on length. The quick start describes clips up to 30 seconds. The create-video reference allows one scene whose script or audio stays within 30 minutes. Both were retrieved the same day and were not reconciled. [HeyGen create video](https://developers.heygen.com/reference/create-video)

AutoShorts is several unrelated repositories. InVideo was not verified from an official page. Do not pick either name as a stack.

## Operators who named a number

Mathis Bolt, on the 1of10 Podcast of 8 May 2026, said his highest month from YouTube Shorts was about $40,000 to $50,000. He also said he had about 2.3 million YouTube subscribers and over 3 billion short-form views in two years. The format he described is about one minute, other people's footage recut into a story, glowing motion-blur captions, an AI voice, and no face cam. The story test he uses is a hero who wants something, something in the way, and what life looks like if they fail. He posts the same file to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, on a stretch of every day or two. He said TikTok pushes a video for about two or three days and then it dies, while old YouTube Shorts keep getting views. He said YouTube pays by far the best. In an earlier interview he put his YouTube rate at about 8, then 15, then 20, and usually about 30 Canadian cents per 1,000 views, and he guessed that a stopped catalog might still make about $300 a day. Those rates and the $40,000 to $50,000 month are his claims. By May 2026 he had a clip finder and editors. [1of10 Podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1bDoeD1Lzgo1se3g6P9kmt) [Bolt interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DehWFTu7yMo)

Tim Danalov, in a YouTube interview, described a faceless Shorts channel, Art of War, posted about once every two days. The first videos stuck near 15,000 views until the seventh. The host, reading the guest's screen, said $260,000 for the year. The video title says $250,000. Danalov also said that at about 80 to 100 million views a month he made about $6,000 to $7,000 while paying a freelancer about $4,000 to $6,000 for 15 videos, so he was not keeping a profit until roughly 100 to 120 million views a month. The expensive part he named was 3D animation. His course pages claim a $700,000-a-year portfolio and, for Art of War, over $300,000 in AdSense in 400 days. Treat the course pages as sales claims. The interview figures are still one person reading his own analytics. [Danalov interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y7yXOTVrTRQ) [Art of War course page](https://theartofyt.com/register)

Zack D. Films has an outside view count and no disclosed profit. Tubefilter, using GospelStats, reported 549.2 million weekly views and 19.3 million subscribers for the week of 9 Feb 2025. A September 2023 Tubefilter piece described original narrated explainers. The 2025 piece calls the channel a content aggregator. An animator's portfolio says his 3D shorts for the channel passed 3 billion views. No Zack or YouTube revenue number was in these sources. [Tubefilter, 10 Feb 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/02/10/top-50-most-viewed-us-youtube-channels-week-of-02-09-2025/) [Tubefilter, 19 Sep 2023](https://www.tubefilter.com/2023/09/19/top-50-most-viewed-us-youtube-channels-week-of-09-17-2023/)

TheSoul Publishing announced that its short-form portfolio had passed 100 billion views in 2024 to 23 Aug. A 2025 Business Insider piece quotes a company executive on packaging existing videos for TV rather than changing production. No 2024 to 2026 dollar profit for 5-Minute Crafts was on the pages opened. [Cyprus Business News](https://www.cbn.com.cy/article/106964/thesoul-s-short-form-content-portfolio-racks-up-100-billion-views-in-2024-so-far) [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/youtube-creator-strategies-for-dominating-tvs-increasing-ad-revenue-2025-3)

An undated Submagic interview with Dr. Philou claims about €300 in a first TikTok monetization month and about €800 in affiliate commissions. The page is a vendor interview with no date. Do not plan against it. [Submagic interview](https://www.submagic.co/blog/submagic-interview-with-dr-filou)

Inference from Danalov's two figures. About $6,000 to $7,000 on about 80 million views is roughly 8 to 9 cents per thousand views. He did not state that rate. It is division of the numbers he gave, and it matches the shape of Bolt's early cents-per-thousand story more than a sponsorship business.

## Synthetic presenters

The sourced money for a synthetic person speaking a sales script is Luo Yonghao's digital twin on a Baidu livestream on 15 June 2025. Baidu told CNBC the session did 55 million yuan. The on-screen people were copies of two real hosts, trained on their old videos. A company press release adds the "we beat the human hour" comparison. CNBC's body does not publish the human stream's gross merchandise value. The same interview says Douyin restricts virtual hosts who do not interact with viewers. No date was given for that rule, and the session was not on Douyin. [CNBC, 19 June 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/19/ai-humans-in-china-just-proved-they-are-better-influencers.html)

Aitana López is an Instagram photo persona. Public income figures conflict. Fortune in November 2023 reported an agency self-report of about €3,000 in a typical month. Infobae on 9 Oct 2026 reported more than €4,000 a month on Fanvue and 404,000 Instagram followers. A May 2026 tabloid reported a spokesperson estimate of about $50,000 to $80,000 a month. The articles that price her describe ads and photos, not a spoken Reel. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/europe/2023/11/23/spanish-influencer-agency-earned-11000-ai-model-posers/) [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/tecno/2026/10/09/una-influencer-factura-mas-de-4000-euros-al-mes-por-fotos-en-lenceria-aitana-lopez-no-existe/)

Neuro-sama's public numbers are Twitch subscriptions and Bits. PLAVE's public numbers are albums, goods, and concerts by human performers in virtual bodies. Neither is a scripted short-form ad channel.

YouTube's blog of 27 May 2026 says a disclosure label alone does not change recommendations or whether a video can earn. The Help Center note of 15 July 2025 renamed repetitious content to inauthentic content. Mass-produced or templated videos stay ineligible for the Partner Program, including slideshows that share the same narration and generic AI with no original insight. Using AI is not itself the ban. [YouTube AI labels](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-viewers-creators/) [YouTube monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)

Instagram's creator post of 31 Aug 2026 says an unlabeled profile that Instagram detects as an AI person can lose recommendations until it adds the "AI-generated profile" label. Profiles that add the label are told their reach will not change because of the label. [Instagram AI profile label](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label)

## What the platforms pay

TikTok's live program on its 2026 pages is Creator Rewards, not the older Creator Fund. The eligibility article was marked updated 18 Aug 2026. A creator must be 18 or older, in one of eight countries (the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, or Brazil), with at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 video views in 30 days. A rewarded video must be original, longer than one minute, and reach at least 1,000 qualified For You views. Duets, Stitches, photos, and sponsored content do not qualify. TikTok does not publish an RPM. A support FAQ says a $10 cash-out floor. The US terms snippet says $50, and amounts under the threshold may expire. The full US legal page timed out, so do not pick a floor until the account's own dashboard is open. [Creator Rewards](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program) [Rewards calculation FAQ](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7543897461245450808) [US Creator Rewards terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en)

YouTube's current Partner Program path for Shorts is 1,000 subscribers and 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days, or the long-form watch-hour path. Shorts ads pay 45% of the creator's share of a pool, after a music split that can cut the creator pool to a half or a third. From 1 Feb 2027, a month of Shorts pool pay requires 10 million qualified views in the prior 90 days. Missing that does not remove the channel from the program. YouTube does not publish an RPM. [Shorts monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220) [Partner Program changes](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009)

Instagram has no general Reels rate on the pages opened. Bonuses are invite-only. The help center requires Japan, South Korea, or the United States, plus 5 million views in the previous three months for the Reels bonus, and it excludes branded content and aggregators. A creator page says the Reels bonus is only in the United States and South Korea. Eligible pieces can earn for up to 28 days. No public RPM is stated. [Instagram bonus rules](https://help.instagram.com/708013994693013) [Earn with Bonuses](https://creators.instagram.com/earn-money/bonuses?locale=en_US)

No 2025 or 2026 study with a stated method measured short-video purchases by bio link, comment, or on-screen call to action. Vendor anecdotes and a 2018 YouTube and Pinterest paper are not a rate to plan against. Clicks exist only if the lab's own redirect records them. [2018 affiliate disclosure study](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1803.08488)

## Craft that is safe to encode

Platform pages tell creators to open in the first few seconds. Instagram's 26 Feb 2025 post says the first 3 seconds should stand alone for non-followers, and it defines View Rate as the share who keep watching after those 3 seconds. TikTok's storytelling article, fetched only as a search extract because the live page timed out, says the first 5 seconds and lists a question, the finished result, a time box, "best saved for last," or a climax-first open. No lift is measured. YouTube says the first ranking question is whether someone chooses to watch, and then it uses average view duration and average percent viewed. It also says there is no minimum posting cadence. [Instagram, 26 Feb 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through?locale=en_US) [YouTube Shorts discovery](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en)

Length is a conflict, not a tip. From 15 Oct 2024, YouTube counts square or vertical uploads up to 3 minutes as Shorts. A copyright claim on a Short over 1 minute blocks it globally. A 2025 survey experiment found self-rated engagement peaking at 34.69 seconds, or 44.09 seconds with music. That is stated engagement, not platform watch time. A small reanalysis of science TikToks found longer videos retaining a smaller share. TikTok's own analytics note says finish rate moves with length, so completion is not comparable across durations. [Three-minute Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en) [Reichstein and Dost, 2025](https://archives.marketing-trends-congress.com/2025/pages/PDF/060.pdf)

Burned-in captions are recommended by TikTok's storytelling article and were not significant in Sheynin's 2025 ANOVA. Treat on-screen text as a factor, not a proven lift. [Sheynin, 2025](http://www.theyoungresearcher.com/papers/sheynin.pdf)

On 18 Dec 2025 Adam Mosseri capped Instagram hashtags at five. He said specific tags help search and do not increase reach. A same-day Instagram quote says fewer targeted tags can improve performance. Both reject a long generic list. [The Verge, 18 Dec 2025](https://www.theverge.com/news/847739/instagram-hashtag-spam-limit)

Instagram's 30 Apr 2024 originality post says recommendation eligibility includes no visible watermark, and that identical copies lose to the original unless the copy is materially changed. A clean master uploaded once per platform is the documented path. Downloading a published TikTok and re-uploading that file is the path those rules describe as a copy. [Instagram originality](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/recommendations-and-originality)

## What this changes in the lab

These are inferences from the sources above. They are the decisions I would lock before writing code.

1. Keep approval on every file. The post APIs fire when called. Until a TikTok app is audited, a public post is the manual pack, because unaudited clients are limited to private posts.

2. Split length by money path. A Creator Rewards video has to be longer than one minute, so the 45-second spoken target cannot earn that program. An affiliate or own-offer video can stay short. Log average watch time and percent viewed. A longer cut can win on seconds and lose on percent at the same time.

3. Score the three money paths apart. A sponsored TikTok, or one built to draw ad clicks, does not qualify for Creator Rewards, and a profile that holds contact details can be ineligible. Judge affiliate and own-offer rows on the lab's redirect and confirmed revenue. Pull estimated revenue from YouTube when the channel is in the partner program. Enter TikTok and Instagram payouts by hand.

4. A 7-day window can compare views and the lab's own clicks. It cannot see TikTok's following-month settlement, YouTube's monthly pool, Instagram bonus days 8 through 28, refunds, or a later channel-level loss of monetization. Do not crown a winner at day 7 after moving several factors. Keep the factor list on the proposal. The results screen should say the cell is too thin to name a cause. Adobe's and Evan Miller's warnings are about web tests, not Shorts, and the failure mode is the same. Stopping when a result first looks good inflates false winners. [Adobe on sample size](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/target/using/activities/abtest/sample-size-determination) [Evan Miller, sequential testing](https://www.evanmiller.org/sequential-ab-testing.html)

5. Faceless YouTube is the path with prior operator-claimed revenue. The avatar arm stays, because comparing the two styles was the point, and Danalov's 3D bill is the warning. An expensive render can erase cents-per-thousand. The daily render cap is a cost control, not a growth setting.

6. Upload a clean file to each platform. Do not export a TikTok watermark onto Reels.

7. Set the AI fields the APIs already expose, and label an avatar profile on Instagram. YouTube says the label is not the earnings penalty. The penalty they named is a channel of interchangeable templates. The improver should not propose near-copies that share a narration and a look. "Try many variants" still needs a visible original point in each script, or the channel matches the 15 July 2025 inauthentic examples.

8. Put TikTok's hook list in the catalog as labels, not as proven winners. Question, end result, time box, saved for last, climax first. Hashtag count can be a column. Five is Instagram's cap, not a reach boost.

9. Bolt and Danalov described posting every day or two. YouTube says no minimum cadence is required. Leave cadence as a setting on the account, not a rule in the ranker.
