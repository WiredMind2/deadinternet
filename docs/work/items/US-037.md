### US-037: Correct a payout with a reversal
**As an** operator, **I want** to reverse a mis-entered payout with a required note **so that** a typo does not permanently distort an experiment's revenue (today `RevenueIn.amount_cents` with `ge=1` makes every entry permanent).
**Points:** 2
**Status:** Done
**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] Given a ledger entry, when a reversal is posted against it, then a linked reversal row is written with a required note — `reverse_revenue` (store/db.py) writes one negative row linked by the new nullable `reverses_entry_id` column (legacy-safe startup migration `_ensure_reversal_link`, same one-transaction shape as `record_revenue`) and lowers `revenue_cents` with it; `POST /experiments/{id}/revenue/{entry_id}/reverse` demands the note (422 on a blank one) and 404s on an unknown experiment or entry (an entry recorded under another experiment is unknown too); pinned by `test_a_reversal_lowers_the_total_and_keeps_both_rows`, `test_a_reversal_needs_a_note`, `test_reversal_for_an_unknown_experiment_or_entry_is_404` (tests/test_revenue.py), and `test_reverse_revenue_lowers_the_total_and_links_the_rows`, `test_legacy_ledger_upgrades_with_the_reversal_link` (tests/test_store.py)
- [x] Given the experiment total, when a reversal lands, then `revenue_cents` decreases by the reversed amount and never goes below 0 — a reversal larger than the remaining total is refused with a 409 naming both numbers, the same entry twice with a 409 naming the first reversal, a reversal row with a 409 of its own, and nothing changes on any refusal; the total and the reversal insert are one transaction, pinned by the trigger-based `test_a_failed_reversal_insert_rolls_the_total_back` (tests/test_store.py); route level: `test_a_reversal_that_would_take_the_total_below_0_is_refused`, `test_reversing_the_same_entry_twice_is_refused`, `test_a_reversal_row_cannot_itself_be_reversed` (tests/test_revenue.py); store level: `test_reverse_revenue_never_takes_the_total_below_zero`, `test_reverse_revenue_refuses_double_reversals_and_reversals_of_reversals` (tests/test_store.py)
- [x] Given the ledger readback, when a reversal exists, then both the original and the reversal row are shown — `GET /experiments/{id}/revenue` keeps newest-first ordering and returns both rows, the reversal carrying `reverses_entry_id`; pinned by the happy-path readbacks above; the RevenuePanel ledger adds a reversal column showing "reverses #N" on the correction and "reversed by #N" on the original, and every unreversed payout gets an inline Reverse affordance that demands the note and refreshes the ledger and the board on success (web/src/App.jsx, verified by `npm run build` — the dashboard has no component test harness, like the other dashboard stories)
