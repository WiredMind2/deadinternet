### US-023: Saving an experiment never erases clicks or revenue
**As an** operator, **I want** clicks and revenue to be owned by their counters alone **so that** a status change racing a click can never silently erase money data.
**Points:** 1
**Status:** Done
**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] Given an experiment with 3 clicks, when a route reads the row, a click arrives, and the route saves its stale copy, then the stored click count is 4 (today `save_experiment` rewrites `clicks` and `revenue_cents` from the copy, erasing the click)
- [x] Given any existing route that calls `save_experiment`, when it runs, then only status/permission fields are written by it; `clicks` and `revenue_cents` change only through `add_click` and `add_revenue`
